BigNums: how to open it the first time
======================================

BigNums isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so macOS shows a warning the
first time you open it. That's expected, and it only happens once. The app has no
server and no account: your bank data stays between your Mac and your own Plaid
account.

1. Drag BigNums into the Applications folder.

2. Open it from Applications. macOS says it can't verify BigNums.
   Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section,
   and click "Open Anyway" next to BigNums. Enter your password.

4. Open BigNums again and click "Open".

   (On macOS 14 you can instead right-click BigNums, choose Open, and click Open.)

Prefer Terminal? Run this once, then open the app normally:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/BigNums.app


After it opens
--------------
BigNums lives in your menu bar (top right). Click it, then "Set up Plaid..." and
paste your own free Plaid keys. The steps are in the Settings window.

Needs macOS 14 or later. The "Ask" assistant needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence
turned on; everything else works without it.
